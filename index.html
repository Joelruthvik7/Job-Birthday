Lessons From the Forefathers

Spiritual Legacies of Power

King David

Shepherd David became King David. God gave strength and courage to David, a simple shepherd boy, and he defeated Goliath and became one of the greatest kings of Israel.

King Solomon

One of the wisest men in the Bible, lost focus because of idol worship and worldly distractions. A reminder that we must control our gifts with wisdom.

Samson

One of the strongest men in the Bible, lost his strength after revealing his secret and falling into weakness. Every human being has gifts, but we must learn to use them with wisdom.
